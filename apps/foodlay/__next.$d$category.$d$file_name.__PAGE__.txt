**Foodlay** is a complete single-vendor restaurant delivery management system designed to streamline online ordering, delivery operations, and business management from one powerful platform. Built with a high-performance Laravel 12 backend and a smooth Vue 3 + Inertia.js frontend, it ensures speed, scalability, and reliability.
The ecosystem includes a feature-rich customer app and a dedicated delivery man app developed in Flutter for both Android and iOS.
From order processing and real-time delivery tracking to reports and customer management, Foodlay provides everything a modern restaurant needs to run and grow digitally.
Clean architecture, easy customization, and a seamless user experience make it an ideal choice for launching or upgrading your food delivery business.

Experience the full power of Foodlay through our interactive demos and explore the admin panel, website, and apps in real time.
